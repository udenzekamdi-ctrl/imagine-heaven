# Background music

`whispers_in_the_rain.mp3` — the user's own Udio track "Whispers in the Rain ext", captured from a phone
screen recording (the Udio download was blocked). Source audio was 128 kbps AAC (no content above ~16.5 kHz)
mastered very hot (−9.3 LUFS, peaks at 0 dBFS).

Gentle clean-up only — arrangement untouched:
- high-pass 30 Hz, −2 dB at 250 Hz (less mud), +1.5 dB at 3.5 kHz (presence), +1.5 dB high shelf at 9 kHz (air)
- two-pass loudness normalisation to −18 LUFS (true peak now −8.4 dBFS — no clipping in the mix)
- 2 s fade-in, 4.7 s fade-out; looped in the video with 6 s equal-power crossfades and ducked under narration
